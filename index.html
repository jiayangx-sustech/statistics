Project 1

Finite-Smoothing Efficiency and Inference in Single-Index Models

SUSTech · 10/2024 – 09/2026
Undergraduate Researcher · Advisor: Prof. Xin Chen

Non-Gaussian errors carry information about the index direction beyond the conditional mean; Single-Index Kernel Density Regression (SI-KDR) uses it.

  • Quantified the cost of smoothing, showing exactly when kernel density regression beats the conditional-mean bound.
  • Turned theory into tuning and tests: the bandwidth rule cut direction error by 56–74% versus reference bandwidths; 95% confidence sets had 93.8–96.6% coverage.
  • Scaled the method to 10,000 predictors, with guarantees intact when screening keeps every active predictor.

Outcome First-author manuscript · JASA · Under review · arXiv:2611.22384 · R package

Project 2

Fast 4D X-ray Imaging via Co-Designed Sparse-View Scans and Reconstruction

CUHK, SUSTech · 10/2025 – Present
Algorithm Lead · Advisors: Prof. Chao Wang and Prof. Jizhou Li

A conventional 3D chemical scan takes almost two hours, and the particle can change during it.

  • Designed a multi-pass scanning protocol, cutting acquisition time from 118 to 19 min.
  • Built a low-rank neural reconstruction model that jointly learns absorption spectra and 3D spatial components shared across energies.
  • Revealed structural and chemical changes over a ~2-hour conventional scan: crack area rose from 23.3% to 37.4%, and the Edge50 shifted 0.35–0.60 eV higher.

Outcome First-author manuscript · Nature Materials · In preparation

Phantom test: 43.7 dB PSNR from a scan 6.3× faster than full angle.

Project 3

Multi-Modal MRI Brain Tumor Segmentation with Missing Modalities

SUSTech · 10/2025 – 04/2026
Undergraduate Researcher · Advisor: Prof. Chao Wang

In clinical practice, MRI sequences are often missing.

  • Developed a lightweight segmentation model that restores missing-modality features without image synthesis, across all 15 modality combinations.
  • Achieved 87.00% Dice on BraTS 2021, averaging 77.01% with missing modalities, and the highest enhancing-tumor Dice (73.17%) among ten methods.

Outcome Sole-author paper · IEEE ICIP 2026 · Published

Project 4

QuadFocusNet: Learning to Route Multi-Scale Patches for Multivariate Time Series Forecasting

SUSTech
Independent project

The best patch size varies from input to input.

  • Designed multi-scale patch routing: parallel tokenizers with different patch lengths, weighted per input by a learnable router.
  • Separated trend and seasonality: a trend MLP and a seasonal Transformer; the forecast is a residual on the last observed value.
  • Achieved the lowest RMSE against five baselines on six datasets.

Outcome Sole-author paper · IJCNN 2026 · Published (Oral)
